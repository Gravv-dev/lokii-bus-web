How we bake

Four steps. Zero shortcuts.

01

Source obsessively

Butter from one dairy, chocolate from one bean-to-bar maker, flour milled within the week. If we can't pronounce it, it doesn't go in.

02

Rest the dough

Every batch sleeps 24 hours in the cold. It's the difference between "nice cookie" and "wait, what is this" cookie.

03

Bake at dawn

Ovens on at 5am, trays rotated by hand, pulled at exactly the moment the edges turn amber. We time it to the second.

04

Box & ship warm

Packed in compostable boxes with a little heat pad, so your first bite tastes like it just left the tray.
